0x72 DungeonTileset II — rebuilt from the real pack

Standalone file:// preview at the game's 1152×768 play size. Pack art is drawn at 4× screen (16px source tile → 64px world tile). The current 1.0.1 art is used only for the side-by-side comparison. No playable file is loaded or modified.

PROPOSAL — NOT APPROVED
real 0x72 v1.7 crops 1152 × 768 fog + revealed color + grayscale topology fixtures hero scale review
What changed from the first preview: walls now use the pack's cap strips and brick faces instead of one repeated 16px block; doors are the real 2×2 leaf with its lintel; columns are the real 1×3 pieces; torches use the pack's animated red wall-torch family. Missing roles are listed in the coverage table, not stamped over the scene. The town panel is an honest stone-gate outpost made only from pack architecture; the pack has no grass, huts, trees, river or bridge.

Focus 1 · the proposal

Dungeon hero shot — fully revealed

Door, torch pairs, banners, wall fountain, columns, crates, chest, potion, sword, stairs, spikes, hero, companion, and eight monster families. Torch light is additive; the room carries a cool ambient tint and vignette so the materials read as a lit space rather than a flat tile sheet.

Focus 2 · same fixture, same scale

Current 1.0.1 art vs the 0x72 direction

Identical 18×12 layout, camera, prop positions and lighting intent. The comparison is about material response, wall readability, actor scale and silhouette, not about matching every prop one-for-one.

Focus 3 · review views

Fog and grayscale

The fog view is an approximation of the existing radial fog, not a new visibility model. The grayscale pair checks value separation: wall cap, brick face, floor, actors and loot should remain distinguishable without hue.

Focus 4 · wall topology

Do the wall rules survive real room shapes?

Six fixtures drawn with the same cap/face rules as the hero shot. Each is a separate 6×5-tile canvas. The cap only appears when a wall has a wall below it, so one-tile walls and the south border do not get a cap on the wrong side.

Focus 5 · town direction

Stone gate outpost — pack architecture only

This is not a grassy village mock-up. It is the stone gatehouse the pack can actually build: an open gate, jambs, torches, banners, a wall fountain and the pack's townsfolk. The current game's village, huts, trees, river, bridge and grass remain custom work; the pack offers none of them.

Focus 6 · proportions

Hero and monster scale against the 64px tile

This is the size decision the design document asked to review before final render bounds. The native 0x72 hero is 64×112 screen; the 3× option is 48×84; the current hero is 64×88. The 0x72 monsters are 32–64px wide and read as room-scale threats rather than small tokens.
