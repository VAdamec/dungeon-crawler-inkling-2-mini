01 / Eight ordinary enemies

Different bodies. Different threats.

Supple slime, membranous bat wings, a lean goblin, articulated bones, a broad armored Orc, segmented Wasp, robed Mage and hooded Healer. Mage and Skeleton use the approved study painters; the remaining six are new designs.

Shown at 2× world scale. Broader/taller silhouettes are proposed art bounds; collision and stats are not changed.

02 / Seven unique campaign bosses + two themed variants · spoilers

More than an enlarged monster head.

A chest with jaws, a three-necked hydra, a charging boar, Medusa with a coiled lower body, a floating wizard, an armored Skeleton King and a winged dragon. The Cook and Bartender have visible role-specific clothing and props; they retain Orc/Skeleton ancestry.

Bosses use their proposed 2× world scale. The hero in each card uses the same world scale as the boss; the biome scenes below provide an in-room comparison. Repeat encounters share artwork, not additional recolors.

03 / Equipment, treasure, hazards and architectural props

Objects you can read at a glance.

All twelve item-table entries are represented, including distinct tome symbols. Up/down stairs, keys, portals and trap states have separate silhouettes. The two plain floor samples are deliberately identical: hidden spikes must have no marker.

04 / Five biome structures + final dragon scale study

Different places, not different filters.

All biome compositions at a glance

Overview thumbnails; the selected scene below is the full 2× gameplay display size.

Prepared 1152×768 compositions, 18×12 tiles at 2×. Crypts have burial niches; caves irregular rock/pools; kitchens ceramic, brick and copper; taverns wood framing/tables/bar; the throne room marble, banners, carpet and a raised dais. Static vignette is design framing, not tested live fog. Stove fire is decorative in this proposal; no new hazard mechanic is added.

05 / People and companions

The lives between fights.

Merchant, prisoner, princess, villager, Marmalade, village dog and chicken. Existing approved figure proportions tie them to the knight. These are front/rest studies; a full directional/cinematic atlas is not yet authored.

Defeated-boss companion size studies, shown beside a full-size hero:

Companion art is normalized for this preview. Future integration must derive scale from logical sprite dimensions, rather than copying the preview painter’s factor into the existing game.

06 / Projectile and magic visual vocabulary

Steel, frost, flame and restoration.

Motif studies only. Spell costs, targeting, damage, timings and cooldowns remain untouched. Animation endpoints and pause behavior require a later approved runtime integration.

Coverage and next review
