Proposed design · color
01 / The village gate

A village, rather than a board of icons.

Broad tiled roofs, timber-framed facades, irregular foliage and a river with proper banks. Buildings are decorative overhangs around unchanged interaction anchors; the road and ground remain flat and overhead.

Prepared art composition, not a generated playable floor. Switch between current and proposed artwork above: anchors, lighting, ground layout and camera stay fixed.
02 / Beneath the old keep

Walls with height. Characters with direction.

Deep wall faces and supporting columns frame a continuous worn flagstone floor. A new knight, robed caster, skeleton and cat share grounded feet and a consistent light direction. Gold stairs and loot stay distinct from the architecture.

Same square-grid play space and ground anchors in both versions. Raised art changes appearance only; wall cutaways and actor visibility need a later approved integration task.
03 / Character construction

The knight, from all four directions.

A compact helmet, visible face, articulated steel armor, red cloak and separate boots. Three walking poses per direction use the same ground anchor; a shield-side silhouette distinguishes the left view from the sword-side right view.

Structure

New silhouettes

Building rooflines, trunks, canopies, wall masses and character anatomy are redrawn. Pixel masks below expose shape changes without color.

Readability

Restrained texture

No bevel around each grass or road tile. Earth, water and stone read as continuous materials, with decoration kept clear of actors.

Integration

Game rules preserved

The design proposes visual overhang for architecture and separate actor art bounds. Collision tiles, progression and current UI remain unchanged.

Compare solid silhouettes: Inn, tree and hero

Solid alpha masks, current on the left and proposed on the right within each pair. Ground anchors are aligned. No grayscale brightness metric is used as a substitute for approval.

Source manifest and scope

Proposed assets are drawn by design.js: building, tree, river, bridge, wall, hero, mage, skeleton, villager, cat, stairs, chest, potion and torch. The current-art view alone uses the isolated ../before-sprites.js snapshot. Proposed mode has no old-art fallback. This is JRPG-02A focus coverage; bosses, other enemies, all biomes, full equipment and cinematic poses remain deferred.
