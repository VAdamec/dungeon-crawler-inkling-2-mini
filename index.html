    Help & settings

    Time pauses while this window is open.

    Controls

    Move / attack: Arrows or WASD. Interact: Space to talk, collect, open a shop or use stairs.

    Supplies: H drinks a stored potion. E shoots your bow. X opens a shop’s Sell list. Choose purchases/sales, then Confirm; Cancel discards the cart. U undoes; Shift+U resets.

    Magic: F Fireball · G Heal · V Frostbolt · B Greater Heal · Z Magic Barrier. Learn spells from tomes.

    Travel: Q/Backspace goes up at ▲. J: Town Scroll / return portal. Enter requests retreat; Y confirms, Esc cancels.

    Tips: Rest at the Inn. Recruit your starter with Space. Switch at the companion post near the Inn. Collect a monster’s key before descending at ▼. Low health pauses the action; critical health offers retreat.

    R starts a new adventure. Esc skips a boss scene or ending.
