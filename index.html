Help & settings

Time pauses while this window is open.

Controls

Arrows/WASD: move · Space: collect / buy / use stairs · H: drink potion · X: sell all gear
Q/Backspace: ascend at ▲ · Enter: retreat · C/Esc: continue from health pause · R: restart
E: bow · F: Fireball (8 MP) · G: Heal (12 MP) · V: Frostbolt · B: Greater Heal · Z: Magic Barrier (knocks a foe back, or slams it into a wall). Learn spells from tomes; each has its own cooldown. MP slowly returns when no fight is near.
Defeated bosses join as tiny companions. They chip in for 1 damage and make dry remarks about traps, retreats, shopping sprees, and rescues.
Find a monster’s key to unlock ▼. Space collects keys. Avoid fire, falling rocks, and hidden spikes.
Potions are stored. Low HP pauses time; critical HP offers retreat. Retreat has a 60s active cooldown. Rest away from enemies to heal.

? opens or closes this window · M mutes sound · Esc skips a boss flourish or the ending · ~ opens the dev console (loadLevel, godMode).
